QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

What does Zypact measure?+

Zypact samples responses to approved B2B SaaS buyer questions through OpenAI and Perplexity APIs. Results apply to the recorded question, engine and conditions — not all users or AI tools.

Does Zypact measure the ChatGPT app directly?+

No. The current OpenAI measurement uses API responses with web search. Answers may differ from what people see inside the consumer ChatGPT app.

What is included in a founding paid pilot?+

A guided scope may include selected buyer questions, recorded answers, brand and competitor observations, cited-source evidence and a walkthrough of findings. Pricing, question count, timing and any retest are confirmed with the team before work begins.

Can Zypact guarantee better AI visibility?+

No. Results are directional and may vary between runs. Zypact does not guarantee recommendations, visibility improvements or revenue outcomes.

Can we examine changes after a marketing action?+

Documented changes and follow-up measurements can be discussed within an agreed pilot scope. A change in answers does not establish that the marketing action caused it.

Is Zypact self-service?+

Guided pilot availability is discussed with the team. This preview is not a self-service account and its assistant uses scripted demonstration replies.
