# Sprint 14B.4 — Positive-case wording polish

## Purpose

14B.3 fixed the major positive-case bug: a strong brand such as Linear is no longer described as a reputation-risk case.

14B.4 tightens the remaining sales wording so positive or mixed cases do not sound like absence/recovery cases.

## Changes

- Replace `Competitors AI recommended instead` with a neutral label: `Other tools AI recommended in the same frame`.
- Replace `lost buying frame` language with `under-performing buying frame` where the brand is present but not always primary.
- Replace `appeared in some unbranded buying prompts` with stronger wording for high-visibility cases: `recommended in most of the unbranded buyer runs tested`.
- Replace recovery-style fix wording with consolidation wording: `Create or strengthen...`.

## Validation target

For Linear, the short report should say:

- `Visibility: 90% recommended...`
- `Branded reputation: positive (0/4 branded runs negative)`
- No `reputation-risk signal`
- No `did not appear as a recommended product`
- No `lost buying frame`
- No `recommended instead` in the headline label

For Vugio, the report can still show a recovery narrative because the brand is absent in unbranded prompts and at risk in branded prompts.
